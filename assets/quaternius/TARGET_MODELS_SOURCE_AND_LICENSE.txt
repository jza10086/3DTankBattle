Conversion (Blender 4.3.2):
- Reused original FBX files already downloaded from official pack; no model was generated from an image.
- Applied source scale 0.36, transformed forward to -Z and up to +Y, lifted the minimum track point to ground Y=0.
- Baked evaluated rest-pose mesh with full original object/armature transforms; removed skeletons and animation. These are static target assets, not replacements for the animated player tank.
- Kept separate Hull, TrackLeft, TrackRight, TurretPivot/TurretMesh, TurretPivot/BarrelPivot/BarrelMesh and BarrelPivot/Muzzle nodes. Mesh nodes have unit scale. Turret and barrel pivots retain true source positions.
- Corrected original imported material alpha=0 to opaque alpha=1; roughness 0.72, metallic 0.15. Original colors preserved.
- Hull includes source merged wheels. Use connected-island convex collision rather than one whole-Hull convex hull, to avoid enclosing empty space. Olive turret includes a tall antenna: likewise do not include the entire turret + antenna in a single oversized convex hull.
- Actual Blender renders visually inspected. Canonical-space full bounds and source/converted hashes are listed below.
